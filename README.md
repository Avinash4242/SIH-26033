## Proposed Solution

### Mediator-Free Smart Mandi – AI-Powered Price Transparency and Direct Auction Platform

The proposed solution is a digital platform that helps farmers sell their produce through transparent online auctions, allowing multiple verified buyers to compete for the same crop.

The platform introduces the following features:

• Live Buyer Auction: Multiple buyers submit competing bids for a farmer's produce, helping farmers identify the best available offer.

• Fair Price Alert: Compares buyer bids with available mandi prices and suggests whether an offer is competitive.

• Unsold Crop Alert: Notifies nearby verified buyers when produce remains unsold, helping farmers find alternative buyers quickly.

• Demand Heatmap: Displays locations where particular crops are in demand, helping farmers identify potential markets.

• Shared Transport Matching: Matches farmers and buyers travelling along similar routes to reduce transportation expenses.

This solution improves price transparency, reduces dependence on intermediaries, and helps farmers make informed selling decisions. Unlike a conventional digital marketplace, its primary focus is transparent price discovery, competitive auctions, and finding buyers for unsold produce.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be used:

• Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
• Backend: Python and Django
• Database: SQLite or PostgreSQL
• Price Analysis: Python and Pandas
• Maps: OpenStreetMap

Methodology and process for implementation:

1. Register farmers and verified buyers.
2. Allow farmers to list crops, quantities, and minimum acceptable prices.
3. Display relevant market price information.
4. Enable buyers to submit competing bids.
5. Notify farmers when better offers become available.
6. Alert nearby buyers about unsold produce.
7. Match compatible deliveries to reduce transportation costs.
8. Build a working prototype using sample crop and bidding data.

## Feasibility and Viability

• Feasibility: Can be developed as a web application using open-source technologies.
• Challenges: Fake bids, inaccurate price data, and unreliable buyers.
• Solutions: Buyer verification, bid validity rules, transparent transaction records, and reliable market-price sources.
• Sustainability: Revenue can be generated through optional buyer subscriptions or logistics service fees.

## Impact and Benefits

• Helps farmers compare offers before selling.
• Encourages fair competition among buyers.
• Reduces information asymmetry in agricultural markets.
• Helps farmers find alternative buyers for unsold produce.
• Can reduce transport costs through delivery matching.
• Improves transparency and efficiency in agricultural trade.
